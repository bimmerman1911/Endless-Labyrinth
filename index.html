YOUR FIELD GUIDE

A little curiosity goes a long way.

Move through the maze with WASD or arrow keys. On mobile, use the directional buttons.

Stand on or next to a wooden drawer and press E, Space, or Search. You can also tap a nearby drawer. One holds the key!

Once you find the key, reach the green exit before your three-minute lantern runs out. Each floor brings a new maze and a fresh lantern.
